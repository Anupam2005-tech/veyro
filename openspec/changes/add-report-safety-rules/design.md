## Context

Unified sections 5.12 and 5.13 describe demo SOS and report privacy. D05 requires the fifth qualifying incident and leaves qualification details to U05.

## Decisions

Use verified caller identity and server-loaded records. Allow report detail only to its reporter and Admin. Resolution notes and the reporter ID are passed to the transactional route for audit and notification. SOS preparation records intent only for the verified trip customer and makes no delivery claim. Incident IDs must already be qualified and window-filtered by the incident integration.
